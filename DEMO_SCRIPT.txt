Demo script — Coffee Records app

1) Introduction (5–10s)
- "Hello — this is [Your Name], demoing the Coffee Records Flutter app connected to Firestore."

2) Show app launch (10–15s)
- Open app home screen, point out "Order Now" and "Coffee Records" navigation.

3) Create a record (20–30s)
- Tap "Order Now" (or +). Fill Title: "Latte", Amount: "2.50", Description: "Afternoon latte". Tap Save.
- Show the new item appears in the records list.

4) Verify in Firestore Console (15–20s)
- Switch to Firebase Console → Firestore → collection `coffee_records`. Show the document and fields (title, des, amount, date).

5) Edit a record (15–20s)
- Tap a record in the list, change the Title to "Latte (large)", Save, show updated title in app and in Firestore Console.

6) Delete a record (10–15s)
- Tap the trash icon on a record, confirm deletion. Show the document removed from Firestore Console.

7) Show rules and config (10–15s)
- Open `firestore.rules` in the project and show it allows read/write (development only).
- Show `android/app/google-services.json` exists and `lib/firebase_options.dart` is configured.

8) Closing (5–10s)
- "That's the demo: CRUD operations working in real-time with Firestore. Thank you."

Tips:
- Narrate each step clearly. Keep the video ~2–4 minutes.
- Record full-screen app and quick switch to browser for Firestore Console.
